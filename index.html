Strategic Pillar 1: South Region Turnaround

Actionable Steps for South Territory Recovery

Fulfillment & Pricing Audit

Execute an immediate operational audit across pricing, supplier contracts, and shipping fulfillment.

Vendor Renegotiation

Target Telephones (-$12,904.99 loss) and Envelopes (-$4,321.13 loss) vendor agreements to restore positive margins.

Strategic Pillar 2: Logistics Optimization

Restructuring Delivery Framework for Heavy Cargo

Hub-and-Spoke Logistics Model

Transition freight handling for heavy cargo items (Tables, Office Machines) from direct Delivery Truck freight to optimized regional distribution hubs.


Expected Outcome: Direct reduction in the 52.2% freight cost baseline and positive margin recovery for Tables (-3.74% current margin).

Strategic Pillar 3: Product Line Repricing

Addressing Underperforming Categories

Tables Sub-Category

Restructure pricing or discontinue unprofitable lines in Central (-$8,115.24) and East (-$10,627.62) territories.

Office Machines

Implement strict minimum profit margin floors to convert $318,169.68 gross revenue into meaningful net yield (>2.77%).

Strategic Pillar 4: Capital Allocation

Scaling Top Performing Products and Regions

Reallocate Marketing Budget

Direct growth capital to high-margin territories: East ($592K Sales) and West ($526K Sales).

Prioritize Core Profit Engines

Increase inventory investment in Binders & Accessories (31.89% Margin) and Chairs & Chairmats.
